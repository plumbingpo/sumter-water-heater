How Columbia Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

Most premature tank failures trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and operate the relief valve each year so it doesn't seize. Keep the temperature near 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard to protect the heat exchanger.

The age of your heater plays a big role. With regular maintenance, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rust-colored water, popping noises, or moisture around the base, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new heater after a leak.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water starts eating the tank steel, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a surprise cleanup.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Columbia

Every type of water heater has its own weak points, and we work on all of them around Columbia. Gas tanks tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks lose heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
